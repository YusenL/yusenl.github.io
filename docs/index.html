About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), advised by Xiufeng Liu, Hua Lu, Angela Huo, and Guandong Xu. I also work closely with Xu Cheng. I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from Sichuan University (2018–2021), where I was advised by Jiancheng Lv, and my Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Tianjin University (2014–2018).

Research Interests

  • Time-Series Data Mining: Unsupervised representation learning, heterogeneous time-series modeling, lightweight and efficient models, and applications to wind power, photovoltaics, and other real-world domains.
  • Multimodal Learning: Multimodal time-series modeling and the extraction, fusion, and disentanglement of cross-modal information.
  • AI for Dentistry: AI-driven screening and discovery of oral-health peptides, together with intelligent methods for cariology and endodontics.
