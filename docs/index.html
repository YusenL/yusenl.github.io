About Me

Hi! I am Yusen Liu, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), advised by Xiufeng Liu, Hua Lu, Angela Huo, and Guandong Xu. I also work closely with Xu Cheng. I received my Master's degree from the College of Computer Science at Sichuan University (2018–2021), where I was advised by Jiancheng Lv, and my Bachelor's degree from the College of Intelligence and Computing at Tianjin University (2014–2018).

Research Interests

  • Time-Series Data Mining: Unsupervised representation learning, heterogeneous time-series modeling, and efficient model design, with applications to wind power, photovoltaics, and other real-world domains.
  • Multimodal Learning: Multimodal time-series modeling, with a focus on cross-modal information extraction, fusion, and disentanglement.
  • AI for Dentistry: AI-assisted screening and discovery of oral-health-related peptides, and AI-driven applications in cariology and endodontics.
